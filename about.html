OUR CONSULTATION PROCESS

A Thoughtful Approach to Balanced Spaces

STEP 01 • INITIAL CONSULTATION

Understand Your Property and Requirements

We begin by discussing your residential home, apartment, office or land plot, understanding your functional goals and spatial requirements.

STEP 02 • FLOOR PLAN REVIEW

Study Room Arrangements, Entrances and Directions

Our consultants examine your architectural drawings, compass orientation, main entrance placement, kitchen zoning and bedroom layouts.

STEP 03 • VASTU ASSESSMENT

Review the Property Using Traditional Vastu Principles

We evaluate the balance of the Five Elements—Earth (Prithvi), Water (Jal), Fire (Agni), Air (Vayu) and Space (Akasha)—across all directional zones.

STEP 04 • RECOMMENDATIONS

Provide Practical Suggestions and a Consultation Report

Clients receive a clear, structured consultation report with thoughtful, practical recommendations tailored to their property layout.
