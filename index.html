SPATIAL ORIENTATION & ENERGY ZONES

The Eight Directions in Vastu Shastra

Every directional quadrant possesses unique cosmic energies, elemental rulerships and architectural functions. Aligning your floor plan with these eight directions creates an intrinsically balanced sanctuary.

NORTH Kubera Zone
North (Uttara)

Governs career advancement, wealth inflow and financial opportunities. Ideal for open spaces, front gates, study desks and light decorative elements.

Ideal: Living, Study, Main Gate
NORTH-EAST Ishanya Zone
North-East (Ishanya)

The most sacred spiritual zone representing supreme clarity, peace and mental focus. Ruled by the Water element; must remain clutter-free and open to morning sunlight.

Ideal: Pooja Room, Meditation
EAST Indra Zone
East (Purva)

Associated with vitality, health, social connections and fresh beginnings. Welcomes life-affirming morning solar rays and natural ventilation into the premises.

Ideal: Main Entrance, Balcony
SOUTH-EAST Agneya Zone
South-East (Agneya)

Ruled by Agni (Fire). Directs metabolic health, zeal, culinary vitality and electrical power. Essential placement point for kitchen stoves and heating infrastructure.

Ideal: Kitchen, Electric Meter
SOUTH Yama Zone
South (Dakshina)

Governs prestige, legal standing, courage and social honor. Requires heavier structural mass and elevated masonry to protect against energy depletion.

Ideal: Bedroom, Solid Wall, Stairs
SOUTH-WEST Nairutya Zone
South-West (Nairutya)

Governed by the Earth (Prithvi) element. The supreme master zone of grounding, authority and leadership. Should be the highest and heaviest quadrant of the layout.

Ideal: Master Bedroom, CEO Cabin
WEST Varuna Zone
West (Pashchima)

Associated with profitability, sustained gains, knowledge retention and children's education. Ideal zone for children's bedrooms, dining halls and study desks.

Ideal: Dining, Children Room, Study
NORTH-WEST Vayavya Zone
North-West (Vayavya)

Ruled by the Air (Vayu) element. Dictates movement, relationships, support networks and hospitality. Excellent location for guest bedrooms and finished merchandise.

Ideal: Guest Room, Finished Goods
