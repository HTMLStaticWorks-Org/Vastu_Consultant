VASTU PRINCIPLES

Five Elements of Vastu

Pancha Mahabhuta Traditional Balance
ELEMENT 01 South-West

Earth (Prithvi)

Stability and grounding. Represents physical structure, foundational strength and enduring spatial permanence within a residence or workplace.

Earth (Prithvi)
Stability and grounding.
ELEMENT 02 North-East

Water (Jal)

Flow and balance. Associated with clarity, serenity, natural illumination and harmonious circulation across living and working areas.

Water (Jal)
Flow and balance.
ELEMENT 03 South-East

Fire (Agni)

Energy and transformation. Governs warmth, vitality, kitchen placement, solar light and dynamic momentum within architectural planning.

Fire (Agni)
Energy and transformation.
ELEMENT 04 North-West

Air (Vayu)

Movement and freshness. Guides natural ventilation, cross-breezes, window orientation and healthful atmospheric circulation.

Air (Vayu)
Movement and freshness.
ELEMENT 05 Brahmasthana (Center)

Space (Akasha)

Openness and connection. Represents the central courtyard or core spatial expanse that unifies all rooms and directional zones.

Space (Akasha)
Openness and connection.
