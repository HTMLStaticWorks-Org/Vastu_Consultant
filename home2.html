VASTU SHASTRA FOUNDATION

Understanding the Art of Vastu Shastra

Vastu Shastra explores the harmonious relationship between architectural spaces, cardinal directions and the five natural elements—Earth (Prithvi), Water (Jal), Fire (Agni), Air (Vayu) and Space (Akasha).

Directional & Elemental Balance Pancha Mahabhuta

Earth & Water

Earth (Prithvi) brings stability and grounding, while Water (Jal) supports flow and balance across key zones.

Fire & Air

Fire (Agni) represents energy and transformation, while Air (Vayu) fosters movement and freshness.

Space (Akasha) — Openness and Connection

The central spatial core connects rooms, entrances and natural light to cultivate a calm, cohesive environment.
