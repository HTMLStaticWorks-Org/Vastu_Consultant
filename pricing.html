VASTU PRINCIPLES

Five Elements of Vastu

Vastu Shastra harmonizes the five natural elements across every residential and commercial layout.

1. Earth (Prithvi)

Stability and grounding. Associated with structural balance, southwest zoning and spatial permanence.

2. Water (Jal)

Flow and balance. Associated with northeast orientation, clarity and peaceful circulation.

3. Fire (Agni)

Energy and transformation. Associated with southeast placement, kitchens and natural solar warmth.

4. Air (Vayu)

Movement and freshness. Guides ventilation, window positions and northwest airflow.

5. Space (Akasha)

Openness and connection. Represents the central core (Brahmasthana) that connects all rooms harmoniously.
